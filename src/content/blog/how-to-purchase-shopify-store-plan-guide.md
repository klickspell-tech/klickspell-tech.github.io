When building a new online store, Shopify provides a trial period to set up your products, theme design, and navigation.

However, once you are ready to remove the storefront password and accept real customer payments, you must select an official **Shopify subscription plan**.

With multiple tiers, varying credit card transaction rates, and billing options, choosing the wrong plan can unnecessarily inflate your monthly overhead. In this guide, we break down which plan to pick and walk through the exact purchasing process step-by-step.

---

## Breakdown: Which Shopify Plan Fits Your Stage?

| Plan | Price (Approx. Monthly) | Best For | Third-Party Fee* | Key Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shopify Starter** | $5 / month | Social selling & lightweight links | 5% | No full online store; link-in-bio checkout only |
| **Basic Shopify** | $39 / mo ($29/mo billed yearly) | **New stores, DTC startups & boutiques** | 2.0% | Full online store, unlimited products, 2 staff accounts |
| **Shopify** | $105 / mo ($79/mo billed yearly) | Growing brands doing $10k–$50k/mo | 1.0% | Lower credit card rates, 5 staff accounts, custom reports |
| **Advanced** | $399 / mo ($299/mo billed yearly) | Scaling high-volume brands ($50k+/mo) | 0.6% | Lowest transaction fees, custom report builder, duties & import taxes |

*\*Third-party fees only apply if you do NOT use Shopify Payments (e.g., using standalone external gateways).*

### The 25% Annual Billing Rule
If you commit to paying annually instead of monthly, Shopify offers a **25% discount** across all standard tiers. For example, on the Basic plan, paying yearly drops your equivalent cost from $39/mo to $29/mo—saving you $120 every year.

## Step-by-Step: How to Purchase Your Plan

Follow these exact steps inside your Shopify admin:

### Step 1: Navigate to Plan Settings
1. Log into your Shopify Admin dashboard.
2. If your trial banner is visible at the bottom of the screen, click **Select a plan**.
3. Alternatively, click **Settings** (gear icon in the bottom left) → select **Plan**.
4. Click **Choose plan** (or **Change plan**).

### Step 2: Select Your Preferred Tier
1. Review the comparison grid for **Basic**, **Shopify**, and **Advanced**.
2. For 90% of newly launching brands, click **Choose Basic**. You can upgrade to higher tiers at any time with prorated billing as order volume scales.

### Step 3: Choose Your Billing Cycle
1. Select your billing frequency:
   - **Pay Monthly:** Maximum flexibility; cancel anytime.
   - **Pay Yearly:** Saves 25% upfront.
2. Verify the renewal date and subtotal shown in the right-hand summary box.

### Step 4: Add Business Address & Payment Method
1. Confirm your legal **Business address** (used for tax compliance and invoicing).
2. Add your **Payment method**:
   - **Credit / Debit Card:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express.
   - **PayPal:** Available in supported international regions.
3. Review the breakdown showing your plan charge and applicable regional tax (GST, VAT, or State Sales Tax).

### Step 5: Confirm and Activate
1. Click **Subscribe** (or **Start plan**).
2. Your account is immediately upgraded, and your billing cycle starts.

## What Happens After You Purchase?

Purchasing a plan immediately unlocks critical launch capabilities:
1. **Remove the Storefront Password:** Go to **Online Store → Preferences** and uncheck **"Restrict access to visitors with the password"**. Your store is now live to the public!
2. **Accept Live Payments:** Customers can now complete live checkout transactions via Shopify Payments, PayPal, or your integrated local gateway.
3. **App Embeds & Custom Domains:** Connect your custom `.com` or branded domain name in **Settings → Domains**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will I lose my trial days if I buy early?
No. Shopify honors the remaining days of your free trial. Your paid billing cycle only triggers once the trial window ends.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade later?
Yes. Shopify handles upgrades seamlessly with automatic prorated invoicing. When your monthly revenue exceeds $15k–$20k, upgrading to the **Shopify** plan often pays for itself through reduced card processing fees.
