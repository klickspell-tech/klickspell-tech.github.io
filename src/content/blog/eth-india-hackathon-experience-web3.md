## The 36-Hour Pressure Cooker: How ETHIndia Works

Hackathons like ETHIndia aren't just coding sprints—they are tests of prioritization, rapid problem framing, and team synergy.

### 1. The Power of Pre-Hackathon Ideation
Starting from a blank sheet of paper when the timer starts is a rookie mistake. The most successful teams:
- Have a curated list of 2–3 problems they care about.
- Understand current ecosystem bounties (e.g., Layer 2 integrations, Account Abstraction, Zero Knowledge proofs, or DeFi primitives).
- Align on tech stacks *before* arrival so environment setup doesn't consume Saturday morning.

## Technical Stack for Rapid Web3 Prototyping

When you have 36 hours to ship a functioning decentralized application (dApp), choice of tooling is critical:

| Layer | Recommended Tool | Why It Wins at Hackathons |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Smart Contracts** | Foundry / Hardhat | Instant compilation, local testnets, and rapid deployment scripts |
| **Frontend Framework** | Next.js / Vite + React | Instant hot-reloading and modular component ecosystems |
| **Wallet Connector** | RainbowKit / Wagmi / ConnectKit | Flawless mobile/desktop wallet onboarding in 5 lines of code |
| **Indexing / APIs** | The Graph / Goldsky / Alchemy | Reliable RPC endpoints and event indexing without custom nodes |
| **Storage** | IPFS / Filecoin / Arweave | Decentralized metadata and asset persistence |

## 3 Critical Lessons Learned

### 1. Scope Down Ruthlessly
A working MVP that solves **one single problem completely** will beat an ambitious, half-finished protocol every single time. If your smart contract fails during live judging or the transaction times out, the judges cannot evaluate your vision.

### 2. The 3-Minute Pitch Matters as Much as the Code
Judges evaluate dozens of projects back-to-back. Your pitch must follow a tight arc:
- **The Hook (30s)**: What is broken in the status quo?
- **The Solution (45s)**: What did you build and how does it work?
- **Live Demo (60s)**: Show a real transaction or interaction on testnet.
- **Vision & Roadmap (45s)**: Where does this go after the hackathon?

### 3. Networking is the Real Prize
Bounties and prizes are exciting, but the long-term ROI of ETHIndia is the network: meeting co-founders, connecting with sponsor engineering teams, and finding mentors who shape your developer journey for years to come.
