Rounding out the comparisons we keep getting asked about — [GoKwik vs Razorpay Magic Checkout](/blog/gokwik-vs-razorpay-magic-checkout-shopify-india) and [GoKwik vs Cashfree Checkout 360](/blog/gokwik-vs-cashfree-checkout-360-shopify-india) — here's the one between the two payment gateways directly: **Razorpay Magic Checkout vs Cashfree Checkout 360**. Unlike GoKwik, both of these are full payment gateways with a one-click checkout layer built on top, not standalone checkout platforms — which makes the gateway pricing itself part of the real comparison, not just the checkout features.

We have no commercial relationship with either company; we set up whichever gateway a client is already using or prefers.

## Gateway pricing: the actual headline difference

This is the most concrete, verifiable difference between the two:

- **Razorpay**: flat **~2% + GST** per domestic transaction, no setup or annual maintenance fee. Magic Checkout itself is separately enterprise-priced — contact sales.
- **Cashfree**: **0% platform fee on sales up to ₹20L, 1.95% after** — and Checkout 360 appears to be included in standard pricing rather than gated behind a separate enterprise quote.

For a smaller or mid-volume store, Cashfree's structure is meaningfully cheaper, especially under the ₹20L threshold where it's free. For a high-volume store already past that threshold, the gap narrows (1.95% vs 2%) but doesn't disappear. Razorpay's flat-rate simplicity is easier to forecast; Cashfree's tiered structure rewards lower-volume or newer stores specifically.

## Address prefill and network size

- **Razorpay Magic Checkout**: 200M+ saved addresses, auto-fill from its shopper identity network.
- **Cashfree Checkout 360**: 120M+ verified saved addresses, with a specifically claimed 95% accuracy rate.

Razorpay's network is larger by the numbers both companies publish. Cashfree is the only one making an explicit accuracy claim rather than just a coverage number — worth asking both what exactly "accuracy" measures before treating either figure as directly comparable.

## RTO and COD handling

- **Razorpay**: prepay nudges, differential COD fees by risk tier, COD disabled for high-risk shoppers, plus an **RTO Protection** product that automates reimbursement when a delivery actually fails — a backstop on top of prevention, not just prevention.
- **Cashfree**: AI-driven RTO prediction trained on 2.5B+ logistics data points, real-time COD allow/block/partial controls, targeted COD masking, claims up to 30% RTO reduction.

Razorpay is the only one of the two with an actual reimbursement/insurance-style product for when prevention fails. If pure prevention is all you need, both have comparable real tooling; if you want a financial backstop on top, that's specifically a Razorpay feature.

## Discounting

Both have genuinely caught up to what used to be considered checkout-specialist-only territory:

- **Razorpay**: Coupon Suite — multicoupon stacking, tiered coupons, audience/list-targeted codes.
- **Cashfree**: stacked discounts, automatic Shopify-native discounts, Buy X Get Y, product/collection-level discounts, prepaid-specific discounts, cashback, No Cost EMI.

Neither publicly documents native A/B testing on discount variants the way GoKwik's Kwik Flows does — Razorpay covers this via a third-party integration (CustomFit.ai); we found no equivalent documented for Cashfree.

## Who each is actually built for

| | Razorpay Magic Checkout | Cashfree Checkout 360 |
|---|---|---|
| Best fit | Higher-volume stores, want a financial backstop on RTO | Lower/mid-volume stores, want transparent pricing from day one |
| Gateway pricing | Flat ~2% + GST | 0% to ₹20L, then 1.95% |
| Checkout layer pricing | Separate enterprise quote | Appears bundled into standard pricing |
| RTO safety net | RTO Protection (reimbursement) | Prevention only |

## What we actually do

If a client is choosing a gateway from scratch and doesn't have a strong existing relationship with either, the pricing structure alone often settles it — Cashfree for a newer or lower-volume store, Razorpay once volume is high enough that the gap narrows and the RTO Protection backstop becomes more valuable. If a client's already deep in one ecosystem (existing settlements, reconciliation, accounting integrations), switching gateways purely for checkout-layer features is rarely worth the migration cost — we'd sooner layer GoKwik or tune the existing checkout than move the underlying payment relationship.
