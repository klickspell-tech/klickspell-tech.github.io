Clients ask us this constantly: *"Before we pay for design, what can we get for free?"*

More than most people expect. Shopify maintains **23 free, official themes** in its Theme Store. These are not trials or third-party templates with tracking scripts baked in. Shopify builds, security-patches, and keeps them compatible with new store features at zero cost, permanently.

This is the page we send clients before any design conversation starts. Share the link, shortlist a theme, then talk to us about customization.

---

## Dawn vs. Horizon: Shopify's Two Free Theme Families

All 23 free themes belong to one of two underlying architectures. Knowing the difference matters more than the color scheme:

<div class="table-scroll"><table>
<thead><tr><th></th><th>Dawn family (13 themes)</th><th>Horizon family (10 themes)</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><strong>Released</strong></td><td>2021 (Shopify's original Online Store 2.0 reference theme)</td><td>2025 (Shopify's next-generation architecture)</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Editor flexibility</strong></td><td>Good: sections and blocks</td><td>Better: nearly every element is a movable block, including on product and collection pages</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>App/tutorial compatibility</strong></td><td>Excellent: most Shopify app docs and YouTube tutorials assume Dawn's structure</td><td>Growing, but still catching up</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Best for</strong></td><td>Merchants who want the most proven, most documented foundation</td><td>Merchants who want the newest editing experience and don't mind fewer third-party guides</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Members</strong></td><td>Dawn, Craft, Sense, Refresh, Origin, Studio, Ride, Colorblock, Crave, Taste, Spotlight, Trade, Publisher</td><td>Horizon, Savor, Ritual, Fabric, Heritage, Atelier, Tinker, Vessel, Pitch, Dwell</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table></div>

If you're not sure which to pick, **start with Dawn or Horizon**. Every other theme in each family is essentially a restyled preset of one of these two.

## How to Install a Free Theme on Your Store

1. In your Shopify Admin, go to **Online Store → Themes**.
2. Scroll to **Theme Library** and click **Add theme → Explore free themes**, or open a theme's link above and click **Add to theme library**.
3. Click **Preview** to test it on a live demo before touching your store.
4. Once added, it sits in your Theme Library and does **not** go live automatically.
5. Click **Customize** to add your logo, colors, and content using Shopify's visual editor.
6. When you're happy, click **Publish**. Only then does it replace your live storefront.

You can add several free themes to your library at once and compare them before committing; none go live until you hit Publish.

## Free Theme vs. Custom Theme: When to Upgrade

Free themes are production-ready. Plenty of funded brands launch on Dawn or Horizon and stay there for years. Where they start to limit you:

- You need a **layout Shopify's editor can't produce** (non-standard product pages, unusual navigation, custom animations).
- You're stacking **five or more apps** just to fake features a custom theme could build natively, which is usually what slows down your store in the first place.
- Your brand identity needs to feel **unmistakably yours**, not "recognizably Dawn with a different font."

None of that means starting over. It usually means customizing the free theme's Liquid code directly: you keep Shopify's stability and app compatibility while removing the design ceiling.
