# Canonical URL Configuration Checklist

> Setting the Canonical URL on **Dev.to** and **Medium** tells Google:
> *"klickspell.com is the authoritative source and owner of this content."*
> This passes all domain authority and search rankings directly to **klickspell.com**.

---

## 🛠️ Instructions

### On Dev.to:
1. Open the article on Dev.to → Click **Edit**.
2. Click the **⚙️ Settings** tab.
3. In **Canonical URL**, paste the corresponding `https://klickspell.com/blog/...` link below.
4. Click **Save Changes / Publish**.

### On Medium:
1. Open the article on Medium → Click the `...` menu (top right) → **More settings**.
2. Scroll to the bottom to **Advanced Settings**.
3. Check the box: **"This story was originally published elsewhere"**.
4. Paste the corresponding `https://klickspell.com/blog/...` link below.
5. Click **Save canonical URL**.
